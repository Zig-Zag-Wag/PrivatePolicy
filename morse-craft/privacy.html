Privacy Policy

Morse Craft ("the app") is a fully offline Morse code trainer. This page explains how your information is handled.

We collect nothing

There is no account system, no server, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDK. The app makes no network requests and works completely in airplane mode after installation.

Where data lives

The following stays on your device only:

  • Learning progress, mistakes, statistics and achievements
  • Preferences such as speed, tone, theme and language
  • The JSON text you can export from Data settings, generated only when you ask for it

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. We cannot read it and cannot recover it for you.

Permissions

The app requests no camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or notification permission, and does not read advertising identifiers. The light signal is drawn as a screen overlay; the camera flash is never used.

Third parties

No data is shared, sold or transferred to any third party. Fonts and icons come from open-source projects, credited with their licences in About.

Children's privacy

Because no personal information is collected, none is collected from children either. The content is suitable for learners of all ages.

Your choices

Settings > Data lets you clear the cache or delete all learning data at any time. Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the in-app document will be updated and the change noted in the release notes.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on the store listing.
