Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to ID Photo Maker, a purely local, offline ID-photo creation tool. It provides background removal, free erasing, suit overlays, background filling, size adjustment, and print layout. We take your privacy seriously. This policy clearly explains how this app handles (or rather, does not handle) your data.

Data Collection

This app does not collect, store, or upload any personal information. All functionality runs entirely on your device and requires no network connection.

  • No personally identifiable information is collected (such as name, email, phone number, or location)
  • No device advertising identifiers are collected (no IDFA, AAID / advertising ID, IMEI, etc.)
  • No network requests are made, and no data is uploaded to any server
  • No third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are integrated

Photo Processing

Photos you select are read and processed only on your device (including background removal, erasing, suit overlays, background filling, size adjustment, and print layout). The results are saved only to your system photo library. We never access, upload, store, or share your photos or processed results with any third party.

Photo Library Permissions

This app uses photo library capabilities only in the following two scenarios:

  • Selecting a photo: photos are chosen through the system photo picker (PHPicker / Photo Picker), which does not grant this app access to read your entire library
  • Saving results: to write the processed result to your library, this app requests "add photos only" access. It does not read, modify, or delete any other photos in your library

Third-Party Services, Ads, and Purchases

This app is free, ad-free, and has no in-app purchases. It integrates no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or third-party tracking services, so there is no cross-app or cross-site data tracking, and no advertising identifier authorization is required (such as iOS ATT / IDFA or Android AD_ID).

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not collect personal information from any user.

Policy Changes

If this privacy policy changes in the future, we will update this page within the app without further individual notice.

Disclaimer

You are responsible for ensuring you have the legal rights to the photos you process and for confirming that the results meet the specific requirements of the relevant registration, enrollment, or ID-issuing authority.
